# Code of conduct

Be decent. Talk to people the way you'd want to be talked to: stay on topic, assume good intent, disagree about
code rather than about people. No harassment, insults, spam or trolling.

The maintainers may edit or delete comments, close issues and pull requests, and block or ban anyone whose behaviour
they find inappropriate, in this repository and in any space connected to it. They decide, and they don't have to
justify it.

To flag a problem, use GitHub's "Report content" on the comment or issue, or contact the maintainer on GitHub.
